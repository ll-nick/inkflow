# Why Inkflow?

Every presentation tool makes you choose.

**Visual editors** — PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides — give you a canvas to
draw on, but lock your work in proprietary formats.

**Code tools** — Beamer, reveal.js, Slidev — keep everything as plain text, but
you describe layout in markup instead of drawing it.

::right::

### Inkflow gives you both

Draw freely in a real vector editor.

Keep everything in open, plain-text files: **SVG, Markdown, Python**.

The canvas *and* the version control — no compromise.
